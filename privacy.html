MET Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

MET ("the app") is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What the app stores

Your expenses, categories, money-in entries and other records you enter are stored on your own device. The app works fully offline.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives:

  • Your name and email address, used only to show which account is signed in.
  • Access to Google Drive limited to the drive.file scope. This means the app can only see and edit files that the app itself created. It cannot see any of your other Drive files.

The app uses this access to keep one backup/sync file of your data in your own Google Drive. The sign-in tokens are stored only on your device.

What we do NOT do

  • The app has no server. Your data is never sent to the developer.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MET's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

You can sign out inside the app at any time. To delete your data, uninstall the app and delete the sync file from your Google Drive. You can also revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
