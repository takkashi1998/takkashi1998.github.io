MET

A personal expense tracker for Android.

MET lets you log your spending, organise it by category, track money coming in, and export reports. It works offline. Your data lives on your own device, and if you choose to sign in with Google, a backup copy is kept in a single file in your own Google Drive so you can sync between devices.

MET has no server and no accounts of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer.
